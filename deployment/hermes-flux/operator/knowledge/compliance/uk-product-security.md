# UK product security (PSTI) for connected devices (operator notes)

Operator summary for the Flux lab, not legal advice. Verify against the sources:
https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2022/46 and
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-product-security-and-telecommunications-infrastructure-product-security-regime

## What it requires (in force since 29 April 2024)
Manufacturers, importers and distributors of consumer connectable products sold in the UK must meet baseline requirements:
- No universal default passwords, and no passwords that are easily guessable from public information.
- A published way to report security vulnerabilities.
- A published minimum period for which the product will receive security updates, stated at the point of sale.

## Using this in a review
- A product with no published security-update period, or that ships with a shared default password, fails the baseline: change-needed.
- Treat these duties as falling on the seller and manufacturer; the household's own rules (three-year minimum) may be stricter.
