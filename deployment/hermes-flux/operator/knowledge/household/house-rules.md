# Household rules (starter policy, revision 2026-11-20.1)

Operator-owned starter rules for the Flux lab. Edit to match the household; the revision above is recorded with every finding that cites this file.

## Purchases
- Bots and Flux agents never buy, order, subscribe or enter payment details.
- Any purchase over £100, or any new subscription, needs Graham's explicit approval.
- Compare the total cost over three years, including subscriptions needed for core features.

## Cameras, doorbells and audio devices
- Prefer devices with privacy zones or masking so the camera covers our own property only.
- Audio recording should be off by default; continuous audio recording of the street or neighbours is not allowed.
- Prefer local storage or UK/EU data residency, end-to-end encryption, and two-factor login.
- A device that captures beyond our boundary needs a compliance review before purchase.

## Connected devices and security
- Only buy connected devices that publish a security-update support period of at least three years from purchase.
- No device that ships with a universal default password.
- Every account uses a unique password and two-factor authentication.

## Messaging
- Agents draft replies only. Nothing is sent without Graham tapping Send.
- Banking apps are blocked for phone control.

## Retention
- Camera clips: keep no longer than 30 days unless saved for a specific incident.
- No retention period may be invented: if a record type has no rule here, escalate to Graham.
